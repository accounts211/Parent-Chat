# Parent Chat privacy notes

Parent Chat stores account email addresses, names, selected towns, posts, replies, marketplace listings, reports, and optional listing photos in Supabase.

The service uses this information to provide sign-in, local filtering, community features, moderation, and Premium access. Email addresses are not displayed in public community views. They are visible only to explicitly allowlisted administrators for operational purposes.

Administrators must use the minimum access needed for moderation and member support. Admin dashboard access is recorded. Do not download or share member data outside the service. Delete or anonymise data when it is no longer needed, subject to legal, safety, payment, and dispute-retention requirements.

Users should not post sensitive personal information, children’s identifying details, precise home addresses, financial data, or unsafe meeting information. Marketplace exchanges should happen in public places.

Before a public launch, replace this operational note with a reviewed privacy notice containing the legal entity, contact details, lawful basis, retention periods, rights requests, processor list, and complaint route appropriate to the operator’s jurisdiction.
