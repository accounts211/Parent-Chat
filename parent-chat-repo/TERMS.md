# Parent Chat community terms

Parent Chat is a local community service. Users are responsible for the accuracy and legality of their posts and marketplace listings.

Do not post harassment, scams, illegal goods, unsafe product claims, private personal information, or identifying information about children. Do not use the service to arrange unsafe meetings. Report concerns through the in-app reporting control.

Marketplace listings are user-generated. Parent Chat does not guarantee the condition, ownership, safety, price, or delivery of an item. Buyers and sellers must make their own checks and agree safe public exchanges.

Administrators may remove content, suspend accounts, or request information where needed for safety, abuse prevention, legal compliance, payment disputes, or service operation. Admin access is limited and logged.

These are product-level community rules, not a substitute for reviewed legal terms. Obtain legal review before accepting real payments or operating publicly at scale.
